## Playable Content

- Guan Yu with eight-direction movement and a three-stage hit-confirm combo.
- Five production enemy roles: Soldier, Shield Guard, Mauler, Duelist, and
  Crossbow.
- One complete three-screen bamboo Stage with two encounters and a Boss arena.
- Failure/Retry, Boss defeat, Result/Replay, Pause, touch controls, Audio, and
  reduced flash/shake settings.
- Desktop and mobile responsive presentation using one intrinsic 1280×720
  Phaser Canvas.
